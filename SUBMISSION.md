## Features
- User registration and authentication
- Create, edit, rename, and delete documents
- Rich-text formatting: bold, italic, underline, headings, and lists
- Import `.txt` and `.md` files
- Document persistence through Supabase
- Share documents with viewer or editor permissions
- Separate owned and shared documents

## Technology
React, TypeScript, Vite, TipTap, Supabase, and Vercel.
